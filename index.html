26-Day Interactive Roadmap

A step-by-step journey from beginner to professional designer.

Day 1-4: Foundation & Basic Tools
  • Introduction to AutoCAD Interface & Setup
  • Unit Setup, Limits, and Basic Shapes (Line, Circle)
  • Modify Tools: Offset, Trim, Fillet
  • Annotation: Text, Dimensions, & Leader
Day 5-11: Professional Management
  • Layer Management & Properties (Linetypes/Colors)
  • Using Blocks & Groups for Efficiency
  • Measuring Tools, Calculating Areas and Distances, Quick Select, and Drawing Recovery.
  • Professional Printing (Scale to Fit, Layouts)
Day 12-20: Architectural Design Project
  • Complete Understanding of Building Bye-laws and Building Codes
  • Creating Detailed Building Sections
  • Site Plans & Location Maps
  • Developing a Complete Floor Plan (Single Story)
  • Opening Schedules (Doors/Windows Details)
  • Designing Exterior Elevations (Front/Side)
Day 21-26: Structural & Advanced Content
  • Structural Layouts: Foundation, Columns, Beams
  • Reinforcement Details, Slab & Staircase Design
  • Detailed Footing & Foundation Plans
  • Final Project Presentation & Certification
